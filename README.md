# AI Visual Portfolio

A curated collection of AI-assisted visual work by Konstantinos Tsikoulis.

This project explores the use of generative AI as part of a broader creative workflow, combining image generation, art direction, visual experimentation, compositing, and post-production.

## About

The portfolio focuses on creating and refining visual concepts through a combination of AI tools and traditional design techniques.

Rather than treating generated images as finished work, the process involves experimentation, selection, editing, composition, and visual refinement to reach the final result.

## What You'll Find

- AI-assisted visual concepts
- Art direction and visual experimentation
- Image generation and refinement
- Creative compositing
- Retouching and post-production
- Concept-driven visual work

## Tools & Workflow

Projects may combine generative AI platforms with tools such as Adobe Photoshop and other image editing and design software.

The workflow varies depending on the project, with AI used as one part of the overall creative process.
